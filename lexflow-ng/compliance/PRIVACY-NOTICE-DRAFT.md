## 2. What we collect and why
| If you are | We collect | Why | Lawful basis [counsel to confirm] |
|---|---|---|---|
| Anyone who signs in | Email address, password (stored by Microsoft as a hash, never seen by us), display name | To give you an account | Performance of a contract |
| A lawyer applying for a listing | Full name, state, city, year of call, practice areas, languages, a short professional summary | To verify and publish your listing | Performance of a contract; your consent to the credential check |
| A lawyer being verified | The official source we checked, its reference, the dates, and the outcome | To show that a real check was done | Legitimate interest in protecting the public; legal obligation where applicable |
| A verified lawyer | The bank account name, bank and account number, or the address of your own Paystack page, where clients should pay you | So that clients pay you directly | Performance of a contract |
| A client requesting a lawyer | Your name, one contact (email or phone) and the general area of law | So the lawyer you chose can reply | Steps taken at your request before a contract |
| A client or lawyer using invoices | Invoice reference, fee type, amount, dates, and whether payment was reported and confirmed | To keep a record both of you can see | Performance of a contract |
| Everyone | A record of actions taken in the system | Security and accountability | Legitimate interest |

**We do not collect** the facts of your legal problem, documents, identity cards, card numbers or bank login details. Please do not send them to us. **We never receive or hold legal fees**: clients pay the lawyer directly, and a payment status on LexFlow is what the lawyer reported and the client confirmed, not proof from a bank.

We do not use advertising trackers or sell personal data.

## 3. Who sees your information
- **The public** sees a published lawyer's name, state, city, year of call, practice areas, languages, summary and the date their verification expires. Nothing else.
- **The lawyer you choose** sees your name and contact. Other lawyers and other clients do not.
- **Your clients** see the payment details our reviewers approved for you.
- **Our reviewers and compliance staff** see applications, verification evidence and payment details in order to check them. [Number and role of staff; confidentiality undertakings.]
- **Service providers** that process data for us: Microsoft (Azure hosting and Microsoft Entra sign-in). [List any others, such as email or SMS providers, before launch.] Paystack is used only by lawyers on their own accounts; we do not send it your data.
- **Authorities**, where the law requires it.
