# Nigerian market research and launch safeguards

## Evidence and interpretation
- HiiL's 2023 Nigeria Justice Needs and Satisfaction research documents widespread justice problems and reliance on informal and community pathways. A legal marketplace should complement—not pretend to replace—these routes.
- Nigeria has 36 states plus the Federal Capital Territory. A state filter means only search coverage; it **does not** prove attorney availability, case coverage or live dispatch.
- Demand hypothesis: public may value affordable initial triage, visible milestones, trustworthy identity verification, mobile-first contact and clear professional fee information. These are hypotheses to validate through structured interviews.
- Lawyers may value improved intake, conflicts workflow, hearing-date discipline, reusable approved drafting, client communications and commercially predictable subscriptions. Validate with both high-street and specialist practices.
- Important gap: there is no reliable, legally cleared national data feed of verified practising lawyers or court cause lists in this prototype. Do not scrape or infer professional standing.

## Regulatory guardrails
1. The Rules of Professional Conduct for Legal Practitioners (2023): advertising, direct solicitation, holding out and fee-sharing concerns; obtain a Nigerian-qualified review before publishing profiles or charging for introductions.
2. Legal Practitioners' Remuneration Order (2023): minimum remuneration and categorisation may affect any displayed 'fixed-fee' service menus.
3. Administration of Criminal Justice Act and state-specific ACJLs: police bail itself is free, and detention processes vary by jurisdiction.
4. Nigeria Data Protection Act (2023), regulations and NDPC guidance: NDPA assessments, sensitive information, security, privacy notices and vendors. Criminal allegations, family matters and medical evidence may be extremely sensitive.
5. Payments: integrating Paystack or Flutterwave does not itself create a legally authorised escrow business. Keep funds out of the platform until the approved PSP/custody model and legal requirements are established.
6. Platform support never substitutes for qualified lawyer review. A graduate of the technology course is not automatically a practising lawyer.

## Safety gates in current demo
Only fictional profiles and sample matters; no legal-services checkout, no automated rights letters, no real police dispatch, no court synchronisation, no escrow, no live lawyer verification, no genuine client submissions or AI legal advice. Demo edits are in-memory only; exported files require appropriate handling.

## Recommended interviews before MVP
At least: small-practice lawyers in Lagos/FCT/Kano/Rivers, legal aid practitioners, women and vulnerable service users, police-station duty counsel, courts administration/records staff where permitted, CAC professionals, bar conduct specialist, privacy counsel, PSP/compliance partners. Test comprehension in the user's chosen language and on low-cost phones / low bandwidth.

## Product sequencing
Start with the factual directory and lawyer SaaS, **not** automatic criminal dispatch. Complete lawyer verification and professional ethics review before launch. Add secure client communications and document control after identity, access, security, privacy and complaint controls are built. Later extend to emergency matching only with a properly staffed model and real acceptance criteria.
