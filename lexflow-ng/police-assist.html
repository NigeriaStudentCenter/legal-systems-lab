Public learning

Understand the next lawful step.

Plain-language general information, not individual legal advice. The applicable law and court orders must be reviewed by a Nigerian legal professional.

Someone has been arrested

Find out the arresting formation, known station and stated reason. Ask for a lawyer and for next of kin to be informed where required. If safety or health is at risk, seek appropriate urgent help. A relative's account alone does not confirm custody.

Police want a statement

Seek legal advice before making or signing a statement. Do not sign what you do not understand. Language and accessibility needs should be raised with the lawyer.

What is police bail?

Police bail may involve lawful conditions or a surety. Police bail itself is free. An appropriately qualified lawyer can assess eligibility and seek release; nobody can guarantee the decision.

What if a person is charged?

A charge initiates formal criminal proceedings through legally authorised channels. A lawyer can review the allegation, advise on procedural rights and represent the person. LexFlow does not issue charges or file court papers.

What if detention continues?

Prompt legal advice can help assess constitutional and statutory safeguards, the offence, any court order and available judicial remedies. Time limits have legally important exceptions; no automatic 24-hour conclusion should be assumed.

Who qualifies for free legal aid?

Eligibility depends on the responsible provider's rules and capacity. The demo "free" flag represents a fictional provider's willingness to assess a referral, not a guarantee of legal-aid funding or acceptance.

Relevant primary framework: Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, Police Act 2020, applicable federal/state criminal procedure laws, professional ethics and data-protection law. Legal text and content must receive expert review before production.
