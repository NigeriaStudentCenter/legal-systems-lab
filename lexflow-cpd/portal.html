Assessment Centre

Practice with ethical AI-use scenarios, reveal model marking criteria, and record a browser-local self-assessment.

Scenario: fictional commercial dispute

A lawyer receives an AI-generated memorandum citing a case that cannot be located. The draft also includes confidential client details copied into a public AI tool. Describe the professional and practical steps needed before this memorandum can be used.

Illustrative marking rubric (10 points)

Source check against authoritative reports (0–3); flag/remove invented case and correct research (0–2); stop inappropriate confidential data sharing and escalate (0–3); documented human approval before use (0–2). The score is self-assessed; no automatic grading of free text.
