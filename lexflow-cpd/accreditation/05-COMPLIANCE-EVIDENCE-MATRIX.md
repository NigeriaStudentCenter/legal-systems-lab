## Protocol for secure reporting
1. Confirm authorised NBA-ICLE recipient, report template, fields, encryption or portal process. Do not email unencrypted participant identities or SCNs without secure policy.
2. Create an approved session record **after** course accreditation. Link only actual enrolled eligible lawyers after identity verification and a lawful privacy notice.
3. Generate pre-event registration list as required, reconcile anomalies and record secure transmission and acknowledgement.
4. Collect verifiable session attendance plus faculty attestations; reject self-click-only proof.
5. Export post-event actual attendee list and hours **as formally approved**, transmit by the deadline, retain a delivery receipt and support inspection.
6. Respond to correction/appeal requests without changing audit history; document record retention and deletion decisions using the clarified Rules and NDPA.
7. Record any 10% amount, fee ceiling and approved invoice only when actual rules/current payment instructions have been revalidated.
