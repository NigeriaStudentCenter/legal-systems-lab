## Annual administrative calendar template
- **T–90+ days:** choose provisional delivery dates, faculty, venue and financial plan; confirm applicability of Rule 12 and 13.
- **T–60 days (Rule 13 route):** complete application plus fee and materials reaches NBA-ICLE at least 60 days before proposed course.
- **T–30 days (Rule 12 accredited-provider activity):** provide Institute advance notice if approved.
- **Within 48 hours before the activity:** submit pre-enrolment information in the required format and keep acknowledgement.
- **Within 72 hours after the activity:** submit actual attendance and any corrections through approved channel.
- **During the year:** inspection cooperation, annual ethics/AML plan and periodic faculty moderation.
- **At year end:** internal audit of courses, participant records, approved hours, income/remittances, appeals and renewal planning.
