Document I — Detailed Course Syllabus and Training Curriculum

Proposed course: Responsible AI for Nigerian Legal Practice Proposed institution: British School of Outdoor Education (BSOE), LexFlow CPD Academy – Nigeria Audience: Qualified Nigerian legal practitioners and legal professional teams, subject to NBA-ICLE eligibility criteria Format: Proposed online/self-paced learning supported by scheduled qualified faculty sessions; regulator approval for the format is requested. Status: Curriculum authored; remaining media and full expert approval outstanding.

Teaching rationale and scope

Provide practical instruction in using generative AI for Nigerian legal research and related drafting, while prioritising authentic legal sources, human responsibility, confidentiality, security, privacy and defensible practice governance. Fictional case files are intentionally not representations of actual Nigerian judicial decisions, statutory deadlines or clients.

Additional guided and practical learning

  • Five interactive legal-AI simulations with three professional decisions each.
  • Five detailed fictitious practice dossiers with invented evidence extracts.
  • Twenty-five guided investigation steps, optionally supported by the protected Azure AI tutor.
  • Digital workbook tasks and individual lesson checks.
  • Six-section, human-marked integrated capstone.
  • Proposed five instructor-led 120-minute workshops (600 scheduled minutes, which may overlap lesson content and must not be double-counted).

Duration clarification: 440 minutes are planning estimates for authored lessons, not evidenced seat time. The proposed workshops may teach the same subject matter and are not additive for accreditation without Institute guidance. The Institute determines eligible learning and approved CPD hours.

Proposed assessment and completion standard

Formative lesson checks and simulated decisions provide feedback, not verified attendance. The six-part capstone is proposed to be marked by appropriately appointed faculty out of 100, with 70/100 a provisional educational benchmark and minimum marks in legal research and privacy. The full rubric, remediation and appeals process require final director approval.

Source and academic quality controls

Official Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and NDPC GAID 2025; Rules of Professional Conduct 2023; NBA-ICLE MCPD Rules 2025. Every real legal proposition or citation requires later final professional verification, including current amendments, applicability and judicial holdings. Source-based legal education is distinct from AI-generated text.

Deliverables for an approved course

Instructor-approved legal slides/demonstrations and transcripts, interactive workbooks, an authenticated legal AI tutor used only for educational coaching, a verified attendance and assessment record, recorded faculty decisions and an approved course completion certificate format. No NBA-ICLE credits until Institute approval.
