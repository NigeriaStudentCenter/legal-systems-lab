# Document V — Assessment Framework and Proposed Evaluation of Participants

## 1. Assessment model
BSOE proposes an evidence-led approach with distinct **formative practice**, **summative faculty assessment** and **verified participation**. AI-generated responses and browser progress clicks cannot substitute for professional review or recognised CPD attendance.

| Type | Method | Learning evidence | Decision authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Diagnostic | Pre-course questions | Baseline AI/legal reliability understanding | Instructor guidance only |
| Formative per lesson | 20 knowledge checks and workbook tasks | Understanding of source discipline, drafting checks, privacy, governance | Immediate educational feedback |
| Applied practice | Five simulations and 25 guided case investigation stages | Decision trail, exception logs, source mapping and escalation | Instructor formative review |
| Summative capstone | Six-part integrated fictional legal AI adoption proposal | 900–1,400-word suggested report with evidence/risk/role/source appendices | Authorised human faculty |
| Participation | Secure enrolment and attendance validation | Instructor-verified session/participant record | Authorised staff, independently of quiz |
| Completion/feedback | Moderated final outcome | Grading rubric, targeted feedback, appeal/reassessment | Approved assessment governance |

## 2. Capstone rubric
| Dimension | Maximum |
|---|---:|
| Business case and controlled pilot scope | 15 |
| Verified Nigerian legal research and citation controls | 20 |
| Confidentiality, NDPA-aware evaluation and AI vendor risk | 20 |
| Governance, assigned review roles and incident response | 15 |
| Reliability tests, monitoring and stop/go criteria | 15 |
| Board recommendation and planned implementation | 15 |
| **Total** | **100** |

**Proposed benchmark only:** 70/100 plus at least 10/20 in each of the research and privacy dimensions, subject to final course-director approval. Serious errors, including fabricated legal authority presented as verified or unauthorised client-data uploads, require manual review and may trigger remediation regardless of total.

## 3. Evidence and marking safeguards
- Identity and enrolment established independently through the approved learning system before a formal submission.
- Learner submits six substantive written responses through the authenticated portal after explicit confirmation.
- The system validates bounded answer format, stores submission and makes it available only to faculty assigned to the corresponding session.
- Faculty enters six integer marks within defined maxima and meaningful written feedback; scores and decisions are recorded.
- A second marker/moderator will sample borderline, high and low submissions, record any revisions and manage disputes. Exact moderation quotas and appeals steps require final policy ratification.
- A reassessment/return decision gives specific targeted feedback and allows a controlled resubmission.
- The model may tutor but does not issue official summative scores or certificates.

## 4. Distinction between BSOE and NBA-ICLE outcomes
BSOE's own educational certificates are governed by independent attendance/assessment approval checks. A capstone pass alone does not issue a BSOE certificate; neither a BSOE certificate nor any local assessment establishes NBA-ICLE credits. Credit eligibility, approved hours, reporting and certification will follow any Institute decision and its reporting instructions.

## 5. Reporting and records
Proposed data records: verified enrolment; scheduled activity; actual attendance and instructor sign-off; grading evidence; moderation log; approved hours once granted; record of complaints, appeals and corrective actions. Retention will be finalised after consultation over differing periods stated in the MCPD Rules and privacy requirements. The present shared Azure development system must not receive real learner records.

## 6. Limitations for Institute review
The proposed threshold, detailed policy, appeals/reassessment procedure and faculty profile are development drafts. Actual learner/faculty browser acceptance and secure production environment evidence are outstanding. These limitations are disclosed so the Institute can specify the conditions for course approval.
