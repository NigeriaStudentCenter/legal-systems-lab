## Learning outcomes
Following successful moderated completion, the participant should be able to:
1. Explain limitations of generative AI in legal research, including hallucinated cases, missing amendments and unsupported legal conclusions.
2. Map a legal research issue, identify relevant authoritative Nigerian sources, verify reported judicial holdings and record unresolved uncertainty.
3. Review AI-assisted contract, drafting and litigation work for unapproved terms, inaccurate facts, inconsistencies, invalid cross-references and missing instructions.
4. Apply professional confidentiality and a documented NDPA/GAID-aware due-diligence workflow before introducing external AI tools.
5. Create a realistic, human-supervised law firm AI adoption proposal with evaluation metrics, role ownership, security controls and serious-error stop rules.
6. Demonstrate judgment in a fictional integrated case study, with credible evidence trail and controlled escalation.

## Distinct teaching and professional safeguards
- Strictly fictitious invented case names and fictional filing rules; explicit labels avoid suggesting binding law.
- Verify Nigerian legal propositions using current primary legislation, authoritative judgments and applicable professional conduct rules.
- No real client papers, secret documents, personal identifiers or actual legal advice entered into the teaching chatbot.
- Human approval gates for every high-risk document, staff authentication and least-privilege learning infrastructure.
- Faculty grievance, reassessment, retention, data access and training logs prepared prior to receiving real participants.
