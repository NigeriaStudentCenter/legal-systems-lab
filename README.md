# Legal Systems Lab — AI, Legal Technology & Operations

## LexFlow NG — Nigerian legal-services marketplace & lawyer app

A companion prototype for graduates and future verified Nigerian legal practitioners:

- [Public marketplace (demo)](lexflow-ng/index.html) — 36 states and FCT, fictional sample profiles, filters and guided legal issue pathways.
- [Lawyer workspace (demo)](lexflow-ng/workspace.html) — custody-rights triage, matter tracking, draft-review studio, incubator and offline study coach.
- [Product specification](lexflow-ng/PRODUCT-SPEC.md) — architecture, RBAC, entity model, user flows, milestones and launch plan.
- [Nigerian research and launch safeguards](lexflow-ng/RESEARCH-AND-COMPLIANCE.md).

**Prototype only:** No real lawyer verification, enquiries, bookings, urgent dispatch, legal advice, client storage, court sync, AI service, payments or escrow. Use fictional data only. Graduation from BSOE does not authorise independent legal practice. Public listing and revenue models require Nigerian legal and ethics review.

### Important notices
This is educational material, not legal advice or a licence to practise law. It does not carry external ABA/ECTS accreditation. Verify current law with primary sources and suitably qualified legal professionals before applying it. Case-clinic examples are fictional training matters.

The **guided tutor** works locally in the browser. Generative Azure AI will remain disabled until a secured organisational gateway is separately deployed and privacy-tested. Do **not** put API keys, privileged client information, assessor answers, or learner records into this public repository.

Browser storage is device-specific, and learners should export backups of their workbook and capstone. External links and video providers may process data when opened.

### Publishing to GitHub Pages
Under **Settings → Pages**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, branch **main**, folder **/(root)**, and save. After deployment, the expected link is `https://nigeriastudentcenter.github.io/legal-systems-lab/`.

Public Pages content is accessible to anyone with the URL. The separate private educator and Azure deployment packages are not part of this repository.
